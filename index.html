Archery facts

Bullseye Bits

Little things I love about my sport, from the shooting line and the judge's chair.

70 m

The Olympic recurve distance. The gold 10-ring is only 12.2 cm wide on a 122 cm target face.

10 in a row

South Korea's women have won every Olympic women's team archery gold since the event began in 1988.

1900

Archery first appeared at the Paris Olympics, left after 1920, and came back for good in 1972.

Line cutter

A judge's favorite call: an arrow touching the line between two rings scores the higher value.

Form > wind

What archery taught me about business: you can't control the conditions, only your process. Same with a deal.
